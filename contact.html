Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI Crop Doctor leaf analysis?
Our AI model is trained on over 150,000 verified agricultural image samples, providing over 98% diagnostic accuracy for major cereal, pulse, fruit, and cash crops.
Are Mandi commodity prices updated daily?
Yes, market prices are refreshed daily at 11:30 AM based on official government Agmarknet feed APIs and regional Mandi board market arrivals.
Is the GROW platform free for farmers?
Yes! All core tools—including the Yield Calculator, Weather Advisory, Crop Doctor AI, and Learning Articles—are 100% free of cost to empower farming communities.
Can I use GROW on my mobile smartphone without installing an app?
Absolutely. The GROW website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile browser screens, working fast even on 3G and 4G rural mobile networks.
